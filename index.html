Commands are parsed locally and always read back for confirmation before anything is sent — voice transcription confuses similar digits, and 604 versus 614 is one phoneme apart. Placing a hold is one step. Clearing one is deliberately not a voice command; take a defective asset out of service fast, put it back only by hand.

Config is held in memory only and is lost on refresh, so your token is never written to disk by this page. Wire it to a keychain or env var when you host it yourself.
